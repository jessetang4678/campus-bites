About Campus Bites

Campus Bites ranks the places to eat on and around campus. Anyone can browse the rankings, read reviews and see how each place's ratings are spread out.

Create an account to leave a rating from 1 to 5 stars and a short review. You get one review per place, and you can edit or delete it whenever your opinion changes. Logged-in users can also add a place that is missing.

The ranking orders places by their average rating; when two averages tie, the place with more reviews comes first. Places without reviews are listed last.
